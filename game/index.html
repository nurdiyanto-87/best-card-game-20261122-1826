EMBER & TIDE

A duel of sparks and waves.
Outplay Marlowe, card by card.

  • 20 life each. First to 0 loses the duel.
  • Each round both sides play one card face down, then reveal.
  • Higher power wins the clash — the loser takes the difference as damage.
  • Element wheel: ▲ Ember beats ◆ Leaf · Leaf beats ■ Stone · Stone beats ● Tide · Tide beats Ember. Advantage adds +2 power.
  • Play the same element twice in a row for a Surge +1.
  • Cards cost energy — it refills and grows every round.

An original game. No account, no network — just the table.
